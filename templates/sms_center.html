How to Get Real SMS Working
Option 1: Semaphore Philippines (DepEd Standard)

1. Sign up at semaphore.co.
2. Copy your API Key from the Account Settings.
3. Paste the key above, click Save, and send real SMS to any PH number.

Option 2: Android Phone Gateway (100% Free Unli SMS)

1. Download "SMS Gateway" app on an Android phone with unli load.
2. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
3. Start the gateway in the app and paste the IP URL above (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8080/send).

Option 3: Twilio SMS (Global Network)

1. Create a free trial account at twilio.com.
2. Copy your Account SID, Auth Token, and Twilio Phone Number into the fields above.
