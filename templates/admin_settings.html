How to switch to any other CCTV Camera (Brand RTSP Formats)
• V380 / V380 Pro: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/live/ch0 (default port 554)
• TP-Link Tapo: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/stream1 (create camera account in Tapo App → Advanced)
• Hikvision: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101
• Dahua / IMOU: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0
• Yoosee / SriHome / ONVIF: rtsp://admin:password@IP:554/onvif1

How to connect a Custom Domain to Project S.M.I.L.E.

You can use any domain or subdomain owned by your school or division. Choose one of the 3 methods below:

Cloudflare Named Tunnel

Run cloudflared tunnel create deped-smile. Free HTTPS SSL, zero router port forwarding needed!

Free DuckDNS Subdomain

Get a free permanent subdomain like deped-smile.duckdns.org in 30 seconds from duckdns.org.

Cloud VPS / Render

Deploy our Docker container to Render.com or DigitalOcean and add a CNAME record for your official domain.
